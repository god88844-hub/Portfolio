03 / About the maker

Curious by nature.
Practical by choice.

My interests move between polished mobile products, interactive interfaces, native tooling, and validation-minded engineering software. The common thread is learning by building, testing, and publishing.

What I'm building now

MEG explores local-first agent workflows. Aerodynamics Lab and Electrophysics Lab explore simulation-backed engineering with explicit limits on what the models can prove.

  • Products Android apps and small games
  • Tooling Rust-based CLI workflows
  • Research desktop simulation and validation

How I like to work

I care about a visible result, a link people can actually follow, and descriptions that don't oversell what's finished. Public repositories contain the implementation details and current limits of experimental work.

  • Background Undergraduate studies at Sri Balaji Degree College
  • Outside code Competitive boxing and logic puzzles
  • Location Visakhapatnam, India
